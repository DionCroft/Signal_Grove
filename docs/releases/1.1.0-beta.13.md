## Living Grove

- Home follows the phone's local time and calendar. Scenes change at
  dawn, daytime, dusk and night.
- Seasonal colours and vegetation shift across the year. Visitors,
  meteors, fireflies and rainbows can appear.
- The Home cloud button uses optional weather for a chosen town.
  Offline scenery continues automatically. Reduce motion keeps the
  artwork still while scenery follows environmental changes.
- See [Home scenery and weather](../USER_GUIDE.md#home-scenery-and-weather)
  for data sharing and attribution.
