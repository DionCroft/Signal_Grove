## Dark themes

Open **Settings → Make it yours → Dark themes** and choose **Moonlit Grove**
for forest green, **Midnight Observatory** for navy and violet, or **Amber Terminal**
for amber and warm charcoal. All three are built in and work offline.
[Theme gallery](../screenshots.md#beta15-dark-themes).
