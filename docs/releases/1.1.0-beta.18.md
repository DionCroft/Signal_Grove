## Kokoro v1.1 offline voices

On compatible ARM64 devices, open **Voice → Read aloud → Download voice pack**.
After the optional 147 MB pack is installed, select **Kokoro offline** to preview
two English and two Mandarin voices, adjust speed, or read the latest completed
Hermes or Phone Chat reply without a network connection.

## Installing the preview

Open **Settings → App updates**, enable **Include preview releases**, and tap
**Check for updates**. The signed APK also installs over beta.17 directly.
