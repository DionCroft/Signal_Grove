## Shared Hermes chats

- Open a conversation in Chat history and choose **Continue in Hermes**
  to send a follow-up and follow activity in the original chat.
- Review an approval and choose **Allow once** or **Deny** for that
  request.
- Reopen the chat to check a known receipt after reconnecting.
- Sign in to Hermes management and select the owning profile. See
  [the setup steps](../USER_GUIDE.md#continue-a-shared-hermes-chat-beta14).
